# Executive Summary - OneApply

OneApply is a Chrome extension built to solve a very specific job-search problem: most candidates apply broadly, but better outcomes often come from one strong application backed by the right internal connection. The product helps users focus on that high-quality path by combining job tracking, contact intelligence, and outreach planning in a single LinkedIn workflow.

At a practical level, OneApply adds two actions directly into LinkedIn pages. On profile pages, users can save contacts and quickly capture context such as relationship strength, recency, and shared background. On job pages, users can track a role and automatically surface relevant contacts at that company. This removes the usual friction of jumping between tabs, spreadsheets, and notes while trying to keep referral opportunities organized.

The core scoring model, called Referral Intelligence Score (RIS), translates relationship signals into a clear priority score. Instead of guessing who to reach out to, users get a ranked view of contacts as strong, warm, or low-priority matches. That ranking powers both the contact table and each tracked job card, making it easier to decide where outreach effort is likely to matter most.

The side panel serves as an operational dashboard. Users can manage contacts and jobs, update application status, save notes, and export data to CSV, TSV, or JSON for backup and reporting. Import support is included as well, so users can restore or merge existing datasets. The overall experience is intentionally lightweight and local-first, with data stored in Chrome local storage.

OneApply also includes an optional AI assistance layer for outreach strategy. If a user provides their own API key, the extension can generate a concise strategic insight, identify top contacts to reconnect with, and draft a short outreach message that stays warm and credible. This is positioned as an accelerator, not a replacement for judgment.

From a product perspective, OneApply is valuable because it narrows attention to high-probability opportunities. Instead of asking users to "apply to more jobs," it helps them apply smarter: identify the right role, identify the right person, and make the right ask. That is a clearer path to interviews, and ultimately to offers.

In short, OneApply turns a fragmented, anxiety-heavy process into a focused system for relationship-driven applications. The project is already functionally complete as an MVP and well-positioned for user testing, iteration, and future enhancements such as analytics, team sharing, and richer CRM-style follow-up workflows.
